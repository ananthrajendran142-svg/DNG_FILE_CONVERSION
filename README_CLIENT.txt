================================================================================
DGN to Vector PDF Converter - Offline Excel Package
================================================================================

This package converts MicroStation DGN and AutoCAD DWG/DXF files directly into
high-precision vector PDF documents right inside Excel.

100% OFFLINE & SECURE:
- No internet connection required.
- No company firewall, proxy, or DLP restrictions.
- CAD drawings never leave this computer.
- No Python installation required.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extract this entire zip folder to your Desktop or Documents folder.
   (Keep 'DGN_Converter.xlsm' and the 'engine' folder together in the same directory).

2. Open 'DGN_Converter.xlsm' in Microsoft Excel.
   If Excel displays a yellow bar asking to "Enable Content" or "Enable Macros",
   click "Enable Content".

3. Click the 'Browse' button to select your .dgn (or .dwg / .dxf) CAD file.

4. (Optional) Choose your preferred Paper Size (A1, A3, A4, etc.) and Color Theme.

5. Click 'Convert DGN to Vector PDF'.
   The conversion will run locally on your laptop in seconds:
   - A clickable hyperlink to your new PDF will appear in Cell B22.
   - A live drawing preview will be displayed directly on the Excel sheet.
   - Click 'Open Converted PDF' to inspect your drawing immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUPPORTED FORMATS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MicroStation DGN V7 (ISFF)
- MicroStation DGN V8 (OLE2)
- AutoCAD DWG & DXF
